The pack ships with [NMS-89](https://linear.app/nemesistech/issue/NMS-89/sample-ckl-scorecard-for-sales-design-partners) under portal [`docs/demo/`](https://github.com/Nemesis-Technologies/portal/tree/main/docs/demo). ## What is in the pack

- Sample **CKL** (STIG Viewer checklist XML)
- Matching **CKLB** (JSON)
- One-page **lab scorecard**
- Regen **README** (engine lab primary; portal first-scan export secondary)

The sample CKL is intended to open in current **DISA STIG Viewer / Workbench** without schema errors. That is a viewer-open check. It is not a Viewer PASS.

## Product download (your Findings)

To export CKL / CKLB from **stored** Findings in the console, use [Export CKL](/first-scan/export-ckl). That is the product path. This demo pack is a committed lab sample, not a substitute for that download.
